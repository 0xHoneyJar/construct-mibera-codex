## Cultural Context

### Bullas Kigu

Bullas are Berachain's bull-companion NFT collection. The kigu is inspired directly by the base bulla design, combined with the kigu format that is seen multiple times over within this collection.

### Taurus Constellation

Taurus is one of the oldest identified constellations, recognized by Babylonian astronomers as the Bull of Heaven over 4,000 years ago. In Greek mythology, Zeus transformed himself into a white bull to carry Europa across the sea.

### The Milky Way

The name itself comes from the Greek *galaxias kyklos* — the milky circle. In myth, the Milky Way was formed when Hera's breast milk sprayed across the sky as she pushed the infant Heracles away. The milky way is bluntly, and quite goofily, represented by waves of actual milk at the bottom of the piece.

## Visual Elements

The milky bullas mibera wears a Bullas-style kigu and drools, holding a green bong beneath the Taurus constellation. The mibera's hair and eyes are blue, complementing the kigu's orange. The black sky and white stars contrast heavily against the colored figure. There is a wavy white pool of milk directly below her. 

## Justification

The bull archetype connects every layer: Bullas the project, Taurus the constellation, the Milky Way named for divine milk. Arty — who created the original Bullas visual identity — drew this Mibera back into that lineage while planting it in the Mibera cosmos.
