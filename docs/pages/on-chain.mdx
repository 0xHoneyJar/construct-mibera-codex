## Mechanics

The contracts above implement several mechanics that the codex documents in detail:

- **Fractured Mibera** — 10 soulbound companion collections (one per reveal phase). [`_codex/data/fractured-mibera.md`](https://github.com/0xHoneyJar/construct-mibera-codex/blob/main/_codex/data/fractured-mibera.md)
- **Shadow Traits** — On-chain trait uniqueness via keccak256 hashes (102 VM-exclusive traits). [`_codex/data/shadow-traits.md`](https://github.com/0xHoneyJar/construct-mibera-codex/blob/main/_codex/data/shadow-traits.md)
- **Candies Marketplace** — 0.1% seizure mechanic + Mibera holder discounts. [`_codex/data/candies-mechanics.md`](https://github.com/0xHoneyJar/construct-mibera-codex/blob/main/_codex/data/candies-mechanics.md)
- **Mibera Sets** — 12-tier ERC-1155 on Optimism. [`_codex/data/mibera-sets.md`](https://github.com/0xHoneyJar/construct-mibera-codex/blob/main/_codex/data/mibera-sets.md)
- **Archetype Quiz** — Soulbound archetype alignment via tarot-anchored questions. [`_codex/data/tarot-quiz.md`](https://github.com/0xHoneyJar/construct-mibera-codex/blob/main/_codex/data/tarot-quiz.md)
- **The 42 Motif** — Numerological easter eggs across contract addresses. [`_codex/data/42-motif.md`](https://github.com/0xHoneyJar/construct-mibera-codex/blob/main/_codex/data/42-motif.md)
