# Acid Test

## Visual Elements


Venue full of speakers / sound systems, with a drum-set on the right side in the background; two big old cameras are placed in front, from a first-person point of view.

## Cultural Context

Ken Kesey's Acid Tests of 1965-66 were the original convergence of LSD, live music, and communal experience -- the direct ancestors of the rave. With the Grateful Dead providing the soundtrack and Owsley's acid fueling the experience, the Acid Tests invented the template that every rave, festival, and tekival has followed since.
