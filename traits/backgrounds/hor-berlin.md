# Hor Berlin

## Visual Elements


Dim moody room with yellow tiled walls, black grid, and red door at the back. In front is a DJ set.

Dominant colors: Dark grey, dark copper.

## Cultural Context

HÖR Berlin, founded in 2019 by DJ duo TV.OUT in Berlin-Kreuzberg, is an independent online music platform and cultural space streaming DJ sets and live acts six days a week. Known for its distinctive bathroom-like studio, it gained prominence during the COVID-19 pandemic, showcasing diverse electronic music from artists like Rødhåd, KI/KI, and Nina Kraviz. HÖR supports emerging and underrepresented artists, emphasizing inclusivity and experimental sound.

Current generation of rave culture broadcasting itself — a DIY streaming platform born in a bathroom-tiled studio that became a global phenomenon.
