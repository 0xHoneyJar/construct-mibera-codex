# Virgo

## Visual Elements


Deep blue night sky with bright white 4-points-stars connected by thin white lines forming a constellation (Virgo). Smaller stars in white dots are scattered in the background.

## Cultural Context

Virgo, the sixth zodiac sign (August 23 - September 22), is an earth sign ruled by Mercury. Symbolized by the Virgin, it represents precision, practicality, and service.
