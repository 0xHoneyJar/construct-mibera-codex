# fae blue

## Visual Elements

a voluminous hairstyle in soft pale blue; straight bangs with 2 loose ponytails, hanging down on each side, tied with a black hair-tie each.

## Cultural Context

The fae style features a voluminous look with straight bangs and two loose ponytails, tied with black hair-ties, hanging on each side. The name references the faeries of Celtic and European folklore — supernatural beings associated with enchantment, mischief, and the boundary between the human and magical worlds.
