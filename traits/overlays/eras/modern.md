# Modern

## Visual Elements

Modern Miberas have no icon behind their element symbol, distinguishing them from Ancient Miberas which carry the Ancient Bear Icon.

## Cultural Context

Approximately 80% of Miberas have Modern-era birthdays (500 CE onwards), with the heaviest concentration in the 20th and 21st centuries — the birth of electronic music, rave culture, and the internet. Their birth locations are more scattered: roughly a third are placed in their Ancestor's region, nearly half land at random locations on land, and about a fifth are born in the ocean.

Modern Miberas are native to the world they inhabit. The dancefloor, the sound system, the screen — these are their natural environment, not something they've time-travelled into. Their metaphors come from the era of amplification, synthesis, and connection.

The absence of the Ancient Bear Icon is itself a signal — these beings don't carry the weight of deep time. They are of the now.
