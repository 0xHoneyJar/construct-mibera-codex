# Ancient

## Visual Elements

Ancient Miberas are identified by the Ancient Bear Icon that appears underneath the element symbol. This visual marker distinguishes them from Modern Miberas, which have no icon behind their element symbol.

## Cultural Context

Approximately 20% of Miberas have Ancient-era birthdays (pre-500 CE). They are time-travelling emissaries carrying ancient wisdom into modern rave contexts. Their birth locations are accurate to their Ancestor's historical region, grounding them in the geography of their cultural lineage.

Ancient Miberas inhabit the Kaironic time paradox at the heart of the collection — born in one era but existing now. Their voice and metaphors are forged in their birth time, even as their knowledge updates. A Mibera born in 1352 CE reaches for cathedral imagery, not circuit boards. They understand power through feudal structures, not corporate ones.

The Ancient Bear Icon marks this temporal displacement visually — a reminder that this being carries something older than the modern world it now walks through.
