# Carpenter's Plane

## Description

A traditional wooden hand plane — the kind passed down through generations of woodworkers. This trait immortalizes three generations of carpentry and a family legacy made eternal on the blockchain through Mibera.

> "The wooden plane is chosen in loving memory of my grandpa, a master carpenter who worked his entire life. He was highly respected in the region and done his carrier even under the Soviet regime, eventually becoming a leader in a furniture factory. When my father later founded our family furniture company in 96, the legacy continued into the second generation. Now my brother and I are proudly carrying it forward as the third generation of woodworkers.
>
> Grandpa accomplished so much: he expertly restored beautiful Biedermeier furniture, taught countless students the old-school ways of traditional carpentry, and carefully preserved his beloved old tools, including planes like this one. Those same tools now have a place of honor in a showcase in our company's conference room. He was also a true Renaissance man: he taught us how to make Pálinka and wine, was a dedicated beekeeper, and could fix literally anything around the house. Most beautifully, he lived his whole life with my grandma and they celebrated 50 years of marriage together. He passed away two years ago at the age of 82, but his spirit lives on through our family and the values he passed down.
>
> Being able to immortalize this "secret" family legacy on the blockchain through Mibera feels incredibly special and poetic. I'm so grateful you drew this trait and that Mibera created the space to turn personal history into something eternal in the codex. This wooden plane represents three generations of craftsmanship, love, and legacy."
>
> — [@yomammma_xyz](https://x.com/yomammma_xyz)
