## Overview

189 unique scrawl texts across 10,000 MiParcels. Every parcel has exactly one scrawl.

**Note:** The [deep scrawl analysis](../../fractures/miparcels/scrawl.md) documents 206 visual variants — the same text can appear with different handwriting style, placement, weight, and ink color. This page tracks the 189 unique *text values* from the metadata; the deep analysis tracks the 206 unique *visual treatments*.
