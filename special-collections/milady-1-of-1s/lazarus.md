A tribute to Milady #602, which is held in a wallet controlled by North Korea's [Lazarus Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazarus_Group) — the state-sponsored hacking organization behind the Ronin Bridge exploit of March 2022 (~$600 million in damages) and the $1.5 billion Bybit hack of 2025. Milady #602 was swept up in the Ronin exploit and now sits in a Lazarus-controlled address, effectively a hostage NFT held by North Korean state hackers.
