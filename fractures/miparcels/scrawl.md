# Scrawl

> *Gumi's handwriting on every parcel. 206 unique fragments of Mibera lore, inked directly onto sealed envelopes.*

---

## Overview

Every MiParcel carries handwritten scrawl — text pulled directly from the Mibera lore and hand-lettered by gumi onto the parcel art. These aren't labels or metadata; they're lore fragments rendered as physical handwriting, each with its own visual character.

There are **206 unique scrawl variants** distributed across the 10,000 MiParcels. The scrawl is the most deeply lore-embedded trait on the parcels — a direct channel between the source texts and the art.

## Visual Properties

Every scrawl variant has four visual dimensions beyond its text content:

| Property | Description | Common Values |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Style** | Handwriting style — casing, effects, flourishes | All Caps, Lower Case, Mixed Case, Smudged, Slanted, Circled, Underlined, Upside Down, Doodle, Fragmented, etc. |
| **Placement** | Position on the parcel | Center Right, All Over, Bottom Right, Top Center, Right, Left, etc. |
| **Color** | Ink color | Black (204 variants), Brown (1), Purple (1) |
| **Weight** | Stroke thickness | Extra Thin, Thin, Thick, Extra Thick |

Most scrawl is black ink in thick weight. The two non-black variants are notable:

- **Brown ink**: *"Refusal"* — All Caps, Smudged, Underlined
- **Purple ink**: *"Choose an existence beyond material fabrics"* — Mixed Case, Smudged, Partially Underlined

> **Note**: Per-MiParcel scrawl assignments are not yet mapped. When that data becomes available, individual Mibera entries will link back to their scrawl variant.

The **spiral** is the recurring visual motif: honeycomb pyramid, endless spiral, whirl, non-linear pathways. Time in the Mibera universe doesn't flow — it spirals.

The **true names** cluster draws from Ursula K. Le Guin's Earthsea — *"in my lands we keep our true names hidden"* — and from demonology: *"to call up a demon you must learn its name."* Names as power, names as vulnerability, names as invocation. The act of naming is never safe.

The **bear cave** is Plato's cave transposed onto Berachain — shadows on the wall as illusions, the guardians as those who've seen outside. Three scrawls reference the cave directly, making it a central location in the mythology.

**"Await the unraveling"** repeated three times, upside down, smudged — the most visually chaotic entry in this cluster. Patience as urgency.
